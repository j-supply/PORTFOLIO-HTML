Work Hood.

2024.

A seemingly simple apparel item on the surface, however there is more to this hoodie that makes it ready for work. Constructed with ultra high weight 500gsm cotton, these hoodies weigh in at over a kilogram. The double layered hood and elbow pads reinforce high wear areas that I observed premature wear in more standard blanks.
