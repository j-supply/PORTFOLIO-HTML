The Hoso~Boyo Kōgeki 攻撃 ‘Attack’ wheel has been designed as an all-out drift specific steering wheel. The small 325mm diameter results in lower driver steering movement input, with deep concave to match low and far back seating positions attributed to bucket seats. Available in leather or suede finished handles with special motif embroidery to suit a car's colour scheme.
