Yokushi.

2021-present.

Checkered floor mats are a classical Japanese car accessory that never became widespread in western automotive tuning markets. I identified this gap in the local market and have developed the Yokushi 抑止 (checkmate) floormats.

Using high quality, thick loop weaved carpet, I researched and designed the ideal universal shape to fit most makes and models of 90s and 00s model vehicles, suiting both right and left hand drive cars. Yokushi floor mats have been in production since 2020. In this time, well over 500 sets worldwide via joysupplyjapan.com.
