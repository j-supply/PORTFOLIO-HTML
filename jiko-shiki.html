Jikō-Shiki.

2023.

Jikō-Shiki(字光式), which translates to character-glowing type, are backlit illuminated vehicle license plates issued in Japan. I produced and distributed these special ‘69-JOY’ plates as a playful homage to the Jiko-Shiko concept, made from a specially made LED panel with laser cut acrylic faces.

Reproducing authentic Japanese car items for western audiences is a major focus for Joy-Supply, as it is a special niche that is not widely fulfilled.
