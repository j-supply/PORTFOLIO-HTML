Shōwa.

2024-present.

Designed and specified from the TRD style bucket seat used in Japanese K-Spec racing in the 1980s. These reproduction seats maintain the classic 80s shape, built with modern rigid fibre reinforced plastic shells and fluffy flannel fabric. The name Shōwa refers to the Shōwa era in Japan, often referring to the golden age of Japanese manufacturing.
