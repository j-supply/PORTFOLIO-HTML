Knitted Hat.

2024.

This small wearable was designed and manufactured as a part of the Abstraction apparel release in early 2024. The knitted beanie features layered artwork that was digitally painted, including the use of the Joy-Supply logo and logotype. This was only produced in one run and sold out shortly after release.
