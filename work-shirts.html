Work Shirts.

2019-2024.

These mechanic shirts were designed to fill a gap in the market where Japanese style work shirts had not yet been made. Constructed from heavy cotton ensures durability and strength as well as natural flame resistance, making them workshop and track compliant. The embroidery on the back incorporates large Japanese text conventions, as well as iconography such as the shuriken stars and flowers similar to Bōsōzoku style and garments.
