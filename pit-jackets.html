Pit Jackets.

2019-2023.

Inspired by racing staff apparel from the 1990s, the range of Joy-Supply Pit Jackets intended to provide a sense of automotive nostalgia in the form of a functional outwear item. The colours and logo designs draw from the iconic liveries that were synonymous across all sorts of sports. Cigarette companies were particularly notable for this, producing high quality merchandise for racing staff and stakeholders.

Between 2019-2023 I have designed and produced six variants, distributing over 150 jackets.
