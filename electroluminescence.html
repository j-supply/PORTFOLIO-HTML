Electroluminescence.

2020-2021.

Pushing the boundaries of automotive signage, I developed and produced these special electroluminescent (EL) panels that were effectively animated stickers. These were received incredibly well, as it was perhaps the first time this technology had been applied to vehicle signage. The panels could be placed inside the window glass and plugged into any 12v socket, producing a delicate, glowing animation inside the car window.
