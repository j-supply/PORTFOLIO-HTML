Shift Knobs.

2020-present.

The shift knob is the center piece of any car interior. Over the years I have researched, developed and manufactured many different shapes and styles of knob to suit a vast range of car styles and driver preferences.

Ranging from poured acrylic, to milled hardwood, and CNCd aluminum, I have done it all. Since the inception of Joy-Supply, I have manufactured and sold over 1500 individual shift knobs.
