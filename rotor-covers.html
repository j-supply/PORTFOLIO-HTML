Rotor Covers.

2024-present.

Brake rotor covers, known as ‘monkey boards’ or ‘fashion spacers’ , are aesthetic disc brake plates popularized by legendary Japanese drift driver Ken Nomura's aftermarket brand, URAS. Primarily designed for show cars and drift styling rather than performance, these plates sit between the wheel and the brake assembly to mimic a retro, industrial look. They are a classic piece of early 00s Japanese car styling meant to fill out the negative space behind deep-dish aftermarket wheels, adding a distinctive, old-school drift culture aesthetic to the vehicle.

Collaborating with my engineer friends in the UK at LimitBreak, we recreated the URAS design and manufactured them to sell once again. This was the first time they had been brought back to market in over 15 years.
