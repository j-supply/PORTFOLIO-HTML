Grad Show Website.

Semester 2, 2026.

For the final assessment of Front-End Web: Crafting Online Experience, we were tasked with designing and developing the 2026 ANU Art and Design grad show website. This was done from scratch in an HTML environment, and included all graduates with individual work showcases.

My response and final output was a completely overhauled reskin of the 2025 website, using brutalist design elements for a stripped down, atmospheric web experience.
