Overalls.

2022-present.

An invaluable item when it comes to working on cars, Joy-Supply overalls and dungarees protect the user from grease and dirt while also being more classically styled compared to contemporary workwear counterparts. These have been tried and tested over the years and have proved incredibly popular and durable. The design of the overalls has taken inspiration from class mechanic staff overalls as well as some Bōsōzoku style elements.
