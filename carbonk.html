CarbonK.

2024-present.

With many of the popular vehicle makes and models used in drifting approaching 40 years of age, much of the safety equipment found in these cars are also aging and well past their time. Many are fitted with original BRIDE bucket seats that are worn and no longer provide the same support and safety as they once did. There are many manufacturers that still make bucket seats today, however they do not have the same aesthetic appeal as the ones distributed in the previous decades.

I designed CarbonK seats to fill this gap in the market. Specified from the original BRIDE Zeta II, these seats maintain the classic 90s shape, built with a rigid modern carbon kevlar weaved shell.
