Birdhouse.

Semester 2, 2025.

For the final assessment in The Ethics of Making: Design for Reuse and Repair, we were tasked with making a livable habitat for an endangered local species.

Our group was tasked with the Gang Gang cockatoo, with our research led us to designing and constructing this bird box. Made with thick, chew resistant recycled hardwood and lined with recycled carpet to deter insects, our final output was extremely durable. The opening was especially developed to block Indian Minor birds from entering and nesting, with the addition of removable ventilation caps for warmer seasons.

Made in collaboration with: Natalie Miller, Tilly Nielsen, Taffany Mills-Hicks and Jacob Haynes.

More on this project: https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1ZqDuX9YXYEzfmKHAwvObA06835ehQSgsY5SKgv6cLqo/edit?usp=sharing

Gang Gang Cockatoo Birdhouse. Recycled hardwood, recycled carpet, PVC pipe, Sikaflex, ABS plastic, ratchet strap.
