I am a designer and maker studying at the Australian National University, with six years of running my own product brand, Joy-Supply. Since 2019 I have taken products from research and concept through to manufacture, marketing and sale, with over 5000 orders distributed worldwide through my own webstore. My work focuses on reviving out-of-production Japanese automotive parts, embodying the ‘Spirit of the Good Old Days’.

I work across many materials and processes: 3D printing, laser cutting, CNC, composites, textiles, screen printing and electronics. I also have a strong graphic foundation in branding, typography, livery and social media content. Through my studies I extend this into front-end web, creative coding, Arduino-based systems and community-engaged design.
