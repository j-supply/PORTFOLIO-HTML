Gloves.

2021-present.

In a racing environment, gloves are an absolute safety must. Not only do they provide critical fire protection in accidents, but they also reduce steering friction and absorb sweat.

The problem I have with the vast majority of racing suitable gloves available on the market is that they are plain and there are rarely special options that are suited to a particular car style. I developed a range to provide racers with more stylised options to suit their flamboyant cars.

Constructed with flame resistant Nomex poly fabric and goatskin suede, these gloves are approved for amateur competition usage. What makes them unique is the screen printed designs on the Nomex, unseen before on racing gloves.
